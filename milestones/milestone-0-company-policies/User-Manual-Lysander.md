# Onboarding Task - User Manual for Lysander

## My Role

I am working on:

- Mobile development tasks for Focus Bear as an intern.

The days and times I work are:

- Monday to Friday, 7:00 AM – 4:00 PM (flexible).

---

## How I Work Best

I do my best work when:

- I have clear goals and priorities.
- I can focus in quiet deep work sessions.
- Deadlines are communicated in advance.
- My environment is clean and organized.
- The lighting is bright white.
- I’m in a cool, comfortable place.
- I sometimes have music on.
- I have something to fidget with nearby, like a Rubik’s Cube, for moments when I need to refocus.

I struggle when:

- Instructions are vague or constantly changing.
- I’m interrupted frequently or multitasked.
- Meetings go on without clear outcomes.
- The environment is noisy or has a lot of movement.
- I don’t have a clear set of tasks to do in order beforehand.

---

## Communication Style

I prefer messages to be:

- Short and direct for lighter matters or quick updates.
- Detailed when explaining complex problems.
- Calls whenever possible for more important or complex matters.

The best way to reach me is:

- Direct PM on Messenger for urgent or important matters.
- Email for lighter, non-urgent messages.
- Call if a discussion is needed.

If you need something urgent, do this:

- Give me a call on Messenger or Discord.
- Make sure I have your name saved in my contacts beforehand so I can recognize you.

---

## Collaboration & Teamwork

I work best in a team when:

- Roles and responsibilities are clear.
- Everyone communicates openly about progress and blockers.
- Team members respect focus time.
- I have a good sense of what’s happening across the team and everything is transparent.

My biggest pet peeve in teamwork is:

- Poor communication and lack of transparency about progress, decisions, or blockers.

If I seem quiet in a meeting, it usually means:

- I am processing ideas internally before responding.

---

## Focus & Productivity

I stay focused by:

- Using structured to-do lists and prioritizing tasks.
- Blocking off time for deep work sessions.
- Keeping the long-term goal in mind while working on day-to-day tasks.

Things that distract me:

- Constant notifications or interruptions.
- Last-minute changes to plans.
- Excessive background noise.
- A messy or disorganized workspace.

The best way to support my productivity is:

- Provide clear instructions and deadlines.
- Limit unnecessary notifications or meetings during focused work.

---

## Feedback & Growth

I like feedback to be:

- Direct, actionable, and with examples when possible.
- Private for sensitive issues, public for general praise.

When I make a mistake, the best way to let me know is:

- Calmly point out the issue and suggest improvements.

I appreciate it when teammates:

- Share updates on progress.
- Are proactive in communication.
- Respect focus time and individual work styles.
- Respect agreed deadlines.
- Follow established plans unless changes are clearly communicated.

- If I become very quiet during long work sessions, it usually means I’m deeply focused and trying to lock in — not that I’m in a bad mood.
