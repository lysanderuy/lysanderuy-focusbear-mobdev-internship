# Onboarding Task - Cyber Security

## Research Questions

### What are common cyber security threats in a remote work environment?

- **Phishing:** Fake emails or messages trick users into revealing passwords or downloading malware.
- **Unsecured networks:** Public or weak home Wi-Fi allows hackers to intercept data.
- **Ransomware:** Malware that locks files until a ransom is paid.
- **Malware infections:** Harmful software that steals data or damages devices.
- **Data breaches:** Unauthorized access to sensitive information, often due to weak security.

### What are best practices for keeping your devices and accounts secure?

1. Always use strong, and unique passwords for all accounts.
2. Enable multi-factor authentication on all accounts for added protection.
3. When in public, it is better to use VPN specially when dealing with personal or confidential information.
4. Make use of antivirus softwares on all devices.
5. Only grant access to data, information, and systems necessary for work.
6. Never leave devices unattended.
7. Regularly back up sensitive and personal information.

### Why is it important to lock your computer when away from your desk?

Locking your computer or laptop when away from your desk ensures that unauthorized users are unable to access your device. It prevents insider threats, data and system breaches.

### How should you handle phishing attempts and suspicious links?

Phishing attempts and suspicious links should never be clicked. Immediately report suspicious activities and attempts. These sources should be deleted or marked as spam, and in cases where the link was pressed, immediately disconnect from the network, check for malware, and change passwords immediately.

### What makes a strong password, and why should you use a password manager?

A strong password should consist of uppercase and lowercase letters, symbols, numbers, and should be complex and unique. It should also be long (at least 12-16 characters). Using a password manager helps generate and store unique passwords for each account, which can also reduce the risk or reuse making it easier to avoid phishing and credential stuffing attacks.

### Why is two-factor authentication (2FA) important, and when should you enable it?

Two-Factor Authentication (2FA) is important because it gives you added protection. It acts as a second layer of security that asks you personally to verify an attempted login through codes, biometric, or text message. It protects your account even if your password and email is stolen.
