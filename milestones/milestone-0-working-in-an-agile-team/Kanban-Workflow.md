# Onboarding Task - Agile Workflows & Kanban

## Research Questions

### How does a Kanban board work, and how does it help manage workflow

A Kanban board works by visualizing a team's progress from requisition and commitment point all the way up to delivery
point. Tasks are presented as Kanban cards that swim through stages in development. As a developer assigned for that
card, you update status based on current progress, and as your progress increases, the closer you get to the end column
or delivery point.

### What do the different columns on a Kanban board represent? (e.g., Backlog, In Progress, Blocked, Done)

The different columns on a Kanban board clearly show status. Progress tracking starts from left and moves across to
the right. Tasks that are still being worked on move under the "In Progress," while tasks that are finished are updated
and moved to the "Done" column.

### How do tasks move through the board, and who is responsible for updating them

Individual contributors or those assigned to specific tasks move the cards through the board. Status should be
real-time and be updated every time it changes. Project managers also often ensure that the board is accurate and
everything is up to date.

### What are the benefits of limiting work in progress (WIP)

Work-in-Progress (WIP) limits allow the team to not get overloaded by tasks. It allows everyone to focus on whats
present and what has more priority rather than doing everything at once. To limit work means to ensure better
productivity as the entire team is able to allocate dedicated focus on the current tasks that are being worked on.

## Reflection

### How does Kanban help manage priorities and avoid overload

With Kanban boards, tasks or requests are listed on the very left. The team then can identify which among these
should be worked on first following a rule of prioritizing more important or urgent tasks. Being overloaded by
numerous tasks can be avoided by setting a limit on the number of Work-in-Progress items that can be done at once.
This ensure that everyone stays on track and not distracted by other less important tasks.

### How can you improve your workflow using Kanban principles

Following Kanban principles ultimately allows me to be more organized. Practicing listing tasks, identifying
priorities, limiting work that can be done at once, and tracking progress allows my workflow to be more systematic
which also would improve efficiency and productivity.
