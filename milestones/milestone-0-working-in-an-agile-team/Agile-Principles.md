## Research Questions

### What is Agile, and how does it differ from traditional project management

Agile is flexible project management approach that emphasizes continuous collaboration, and adaptability to change.
Its approach comes in an iterative, and incremental fashion, using short sprints (1-4 weeks) to deliver functional
features onto a product.

### What are the core values and principles of Agile

The Agile Manifesto embodies, but are not limited to, these following principles:

1. Prioritize customer satisfaction through early and continuous delivery
2. Be open and flexible for changing requirements
3. Both the stakeholders and the production team must work closely together
4. Build projects around motivated individuals
5. Fostering face-to-face meetings for efficient communication
6. Continuous attention to technical excellence
7. Retrospection is important and crucial for improvement

### What are the differences between Scrum and Kanban, and when is each used

The main difference between Scrum and Kanban is with there specific approach for project management and development.
Scrum is naturally more systamic in many ways when it comes to planning, holding meetings, and other ceremonies. Scrum
is still flexible in nature but requirements are usually discussed and laid out before the start of each sprint.
Scrum is ideal for projects with evolving requirements that benefit from structured planning, and frequent reviews.
Kanban on the other hand is more flexible and continuoes. It uses a Kanban board to track tasks, monitor progress, and manage
priorities. There are no fixed iterations, and planning is done on the go making it more useful for changing priorities
and workloads. It is better suited for projects with continuous delivery needs.

### Why does Focus Bear lean towards Kanban instead of Scrum

Focus Bear tends to lean towards Kanban instead of Scrum because of the flexible and and flow-oriented nature of Kanban.
Its visual boards make it easy to track progress and spot bottlenecks in real time. This approach fits small,
fast-moving teams better than Scrum's rigid roles and timeboxed sprints.

## Reflection

### What do you think are the biggest benefits and challenges of Agile

One major benefit with an Agile approach is customer satisfaction because of fast and continuous delivery. Agile is
also flexible and able to adapt with changing requirements. For managers, Agile approach also brings better control
because teams prioritize transparency. Quality is also ensured because of continuous reviews and feedback and
stakeholders are involved in the process through advanced reporting tools and techniques.

### How can Agile principles help improve your role and responsibilities

Agile principles can help improve my roles and responsibilities by encouraging a more collaborative, adaptive, and
efficient work environment. By focusing on customer satisfaction, I can ensure that the work I deliver
directly addresses real user needs.
