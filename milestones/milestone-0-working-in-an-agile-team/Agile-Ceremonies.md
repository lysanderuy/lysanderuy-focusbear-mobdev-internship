# Onboarding Task - Agile Ceremonies & Team Collaboration

## Research Questions

### What are the main Agile ceremonies, and what is their purpose

- **Daily Stand-ups -** Daily Stand-ups are short meetings held everyday to keep the team in sync. During a stand-up
  each member shares what he has accomplished yesterday, and what he is planning to do today. It allows the team to
  keep moving smoothly while addressing any concerns along the way.
- **Sprint Planning -** Defines the work to be done for a certain task or end goal, usually for a multiple-week sprint.
  - Scrum - Fixed, time-boxed planning usually at the start of each sprint. Goals are already set beforehand.
  - Kanban - No fixed iterations with a just-in-time planning mindset. The team focuses on one thing at a time and
    plans continuously along the way.
- **Retrospectives -** Allows the team to reflect on the current process, and identify any areas that could be improved.
- **Backlog Refinement -** Allows the team to prepare and clarify any upcoming backlogs to ensure that backlogs
  are ready for future sprint planning.

### How do Agile teams collaborate asynchronously and across time zones

Agile teams are able to collaborate asynchronously and across time zones by dedicating a shared time period for work
alone that is available for anyone. A fixed time slot accessible to everyone on the team is also set which allows the
team to hold daily stand-ups, and planning, while allowing each individual member to work on their own
asynchronously outside these hours.

## Reflection

### How do Agile ceremonies help with communication and alignment

Agile ceremonies create a shared overlap period where the team can get back in sync and make sure everything is
running smoothly. With agile ceremonies, the team is provided a structured way or framework for meetings to ensure
that everyone is still aligned on the goals set, progress, and priorities.

### Which ceremony do you think is most important for your role, and why

I think the most important ceremony as a Mobile App Developer would be the sprint planning. As a developer,
it is important to have everything laid out properly to ensure that work is aligned with what the team needs.
Planning for tasks, deliverables and what the end goal is allows developers to code and build with vision,
and not just go blindly.

For us interns, we usually participate the stand-ups by talking about the progress for our onboarding program.
We start by stating what we have achieved so far, and what we are working on at the moment. For our senior devs,
