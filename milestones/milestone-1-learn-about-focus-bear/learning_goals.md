# Onboarding Task - Set your learning goals

## Reflection

### What do you hope to learn from this internship?

I’m hoping to really understand what mobile development looks like in a real production environment. During my academic
years, most of my projects were web and desktop applications, so while I understand general software development
principles, I want to learn how those translate specifically to mobile. I want to experience how features are
planned, built, tested, and shipped in an actual app that real users rely on.

### What skills are you most excited to develop?

I’m most excited to strengthen my mobile development skills, especially in building clean, scalable features within
an existing codebase. I also want to improve my understanding of mobile architecture, performance considerations,
and writing production-ready code rather than just school-project code. Beyond technical skills, I’m looking forward
to improving how I communicate within a dev team and collaborate effectively.

### What do you think you can contribute to the team?

Even though I’m newer to mobile, I bring solid fundamentals from working on web and desktop applications.
I’m comfortable with core programming concepts, problem-solving, and debugging. I’m also someone who takes
ownership of tasks and genuinely wants to understand why things are built a certain way, not just how. I think
I can contribute by being proactive, dependable, and willing to put in the effort to grow quickly and support
the team wherever needed.

### What is one area you feel less confident in that you’d like to improve?

I’d say working in a large, production-level mobile codebase is something I’m still building confidence in. In school,
projects are usually smaller and more controlled. I want to get better at navigating bigger systems, understanding
architecture decisions, and making changes without breaking existing functionality. I want to feel fully confident
in my role as a mobile developer, not just someone transitioning from web and desktop.

### What does success in this internship look like for you?

Success for me would mean being able to contribute meaningful features to the actual Focus Bear app, not just small
fixes but real functionality that users benefit from. Academically, I’d like to achieve a distinction or high
distinction grade for this internship. But more importantly, I want to finish this experience feeling like a capable
and equipped mobile developer and someone who can confidently contribute as a full team member
