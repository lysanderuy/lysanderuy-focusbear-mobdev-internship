# Onboarding Task - Focus Bear's mission and vision

## Reflection

### Why do you think Focus Bear was created

Focus Bear was created mainly because of one reason, distraction. Sir Jeremy built Focus Bear initially for himself.
He had mentioned that there was a time in his life where he couldn't stay focused and got distracted with playing
games or reading emails and only did a couple of hours for work. When it got to the end of the day, only then, could
he start locking in to finish his tasks but time with his family was sacrificed. This problem is not just something
that sir Jeremy experienced but a problem experienced by many people with ADHD or AuDHD.

### What problem is Focus Bear solving

Focus Bear is solving distraction, especially for people with ADHD or autism. It acts as a second layer of self
control that tells the users if certain apps might be distrating or might be drawing them away from their work
or focus sessions.

### Why do you think this mission is important

This mission is important because it helps a lot of people to stay on track with the work, study, or tasks that
they have to do. It allows them to stay disciplined and actually spend the right time for work. Doing this allows
user to better manage time with work and personal life and not sacrifice personal relationships.

### How does Focus Bear’s work align with your personal values or interests

For a very long time, though I am able to deliver quality outputs, I have always been the type to either start late
because of spending too much time online, or playing games, or finishing late because of always getting distracting
during work sessions. I have always wanted to find a way that helps me stay disciplined with what I do, and so
being able to understand Focus Bear's mission and even use the app for my personal needs really allows me to fully
stay on track. It also inspires me that an app is built around the needs of others because helping people is also
something that I want to do.

### Do you personally relate to any of the challenges that Focus Bear aims to solve

Yes, and very much so. I never realized how much distraction I let get to me during my sessions. I only realized how
hard it is for me to stay focused with what I'm supposed to do when I started using the Focus Bear app. Most of the
times I would even forget that I have certain apps blocked and when I get bored or distracted I try to open these apps,
and get reminded that I have blocked them in the first place and now I am forced to focus with work again. I find
the app very useful because it really shifts your old ways and helps you be disciplined.
