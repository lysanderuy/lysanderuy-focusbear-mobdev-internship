# Onboarding Task - Understand competitive landscape

## Research Questions

### Other Productivity and Focus Apps

- **Freedom-** A productivity app that mainly offers blocklisting, sessions, and scheduling.
- **Cold Turkey -** Mainly a blocker app that allows you to keep your focus on more important matters.
- **Tiimo -** A visual planner that helps layout plans for the day, or week. Allows synchronization across Apple
  devices for constant reminder and aids in anticipating what comes next on your day.
- **Routinery -** A habit building app that allows users to create and lay out habits for the day.

---

## Reflection

### What makes Focus Bear different from these apps

Personally, I think Focus Bear encapsulates all of the main features that these 4 productivity and focus apps have to
offer. Focus Bear offers Focus sessions, blocklisting, and habit builting all in one app.

### If you were a user, why would you choose Focus Bear over competitors

I think the main factor would be having most of the features that I need to stay focused for my tasks and have a
structured outline for how my day will go. Instead of downloading separate apps for different tasks, and setting up
these apps individual while trying to use them in sync is too much of a hassle in itself whereas for Focus Bear,
I can setup everything together in the same system and proceed with my workflow.

### What’s one feature that other apps have that Focus Bear doesn’t

Some of the mentioned apps have very customizable blocklists. Blocklists can be scheduled, continuous,
or session-based which would help a lot for different kinds of work.

### Based on your research, what’s one improvement you think Focus Bear could make

One feature that I would love Focus Bear to have would be session-based blocking. Personally, I find it inefficient to
have to set specific time frames for blocklists to be active and when sessions are done before these set schedules,
I would still have to manually disable blocking. It would be nice if I can create specific and dedicated blocklists
with customizable settings for specific sessions and have it turn off immediately when I am done with my work.
